Will it fit, and how fast can it go?

Fit is weights + KV cache against usable memory. On a discrete GPU, usable is the VRAM minus about 1 GB. On a Mac, usable is about 75% of unified memory (measured ≈11.8 GiB on a 16 GB machine). Speed ceiling is bandwidth ÷ model size.
