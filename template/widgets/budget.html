Will the whole stack fit on 16 GB?

A 16 GB Mac keeps about 4 GB for macOS, leaving a usable window of roughly 9–12 GB. The model, the retrieval stack and the KV cache all have to land inside it.

macOSmodelretrievalKV + runtimefree
8.8 GB workload
Fits comfortably.

Planning bands, not a measurement. The KV cache is shown as the 1–2 GB band the sizes above imply, not a per-token formula. Watch your memory monitor on the real machine; on a 24 GB or 32 GB machine the same arithmetic gives you much more room.
